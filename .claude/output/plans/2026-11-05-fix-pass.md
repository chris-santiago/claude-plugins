## User decisions (2026-11-05)

1. **Override of an open choice → ruling round, re-checked by the spec reviewer.** The batch logs the override as a ruling on the task (`append --type progress --task N --note "user ruling: override <choice id>: <behavior>"`) and settles the choice (`resolve <choice id> --note "user ruling: override ..."`). `triage` turns a ruling logged since its *last entry* into a ruling round when the last entry is `escalate` or `done` (today only after `escalate`). A ruling round that sent no finding re-checks the spec reviewer. Doesn't count toward the two-round bound; a failed re-check returns to the user.
2. **Deferring a root cause → C.** A holistic decision doc that lists a `root cause:` concern id in its `excluded` (with reason) defers it: triage stops counting it as open work and files a follow-up (rule = the reason) once. Coders re-list a root cause "until a decision doc claims it (a fix choice's `findings` or its `excluded`)". Also: `resolve` accepts concern ids with a user ruling, and triage drops waived root causes (escape hatch for an escalated task).
3. **A decision doc's `escalate` choice → the contest path.** In a holistic round, a finding the decision escalates is contested by the coder with the doc's evidence (`wrong` or `plan-conflict`); the raising reviewer answers in the round's all-reviewer re-check (concede closes, hold goes to the batch). Per-task scopes stop blocking `ready` on decision escalation items (the contest/held mechanics govern); `final`/close-gate scopes keep the existing escalation blocker.
4. **Unplanned contract change → open choice.** A contract change the plan didn't specify (first attempt or a holistic decision) is implemented and recorded in `open`, parks for the batch, default accept; an override is a ruling round (decision 1). Replace every "escalate public API changes before implementing" in coder docs; drop the "contract change needing approval" ready-blocker claim from SKILL.md and docs.
